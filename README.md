# SNOM_JCM

Scanning Near-Field Optical Miscroscopy (SNOM) is a technique that "beats" the diffraction limit through near field excitation at a sample. SNOM can achieve nanometer resolution of both topological and optical information.

In the McPeak lab, we perform nanoFTIR experiments on nanomaterials to study local spatial effects on interfacial physics and surface chemistry. Currently, we are developing methods to perform nanoFTIR measurements through a silicon nitride window into flowing water. Our aim is to advance foundational experimentation and theory of nanoFTIR methods. 

I lead efforts for simulations of nanoFTIR systems. My primary goal is providing ideal results to support experimental data. Additionally, I design simulations to produce various effects that our nanoFTIR experiments are discovering about the nanoscale interface between water and silicon nitride.
